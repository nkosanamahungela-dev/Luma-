<h1 align="center">Luma</h1>

<p align="center">Every moment, beautifully kept.</p>

Luma is a beautiful, modern gallery for all your photos and videos. Enjoy a clean, elegant experience designed to make browsing and organising your memories effortless.

## Features

- Browse photos and videos by date
- Organise media with albums and favourites
- Search photos by name, date, or tag
- Edit photos and videos offline
- Keep private media in a secure folder
- Manage deleted items with the trash bin
- View detailed information about your media
- Connect to supported cloud and network storage
- Customise the look and feel of your gallery
- Privacy-focused experience
